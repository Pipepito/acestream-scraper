## Design Context

### Users

AceStream Scraper is primarily used by a single self-hosting operator managing channels, scrapers, playback, integrations, and system health. Some users have limited Docker or networking knowledge. Interfaces should help them complete operational tasks confidently without requiring them to understand image internals, while retaining precise advanced controls for experienced operators.

### Brand Personality

Bold, powerful, operational, direct, and supportive. The product should feel dependable and actively helpful: it explains consequences in plain language, makes the next decision obvious, and avoids decorative complexity that competes with system state.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a measured-bold operational style rather than a generic admin template. Keep IBM Plex Sans, compact information density, teal/blue structural color, and restrained warm accents for attention. Preserve equivalent hierarchy in light and dark themes, strong focus treatment, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion behavior. Avoid glassmorphism, neon styling, purple gradients, novelty motion, and interchangeable card grids.

### Design Principles

1. Show only controls that can affect the current configuration; explain unavailable capabilities at the point where the user chooses a platform or image.
2. Lead with the primary operational decision and progressively disclose advanced settings.
3. Use plain language and keep guidance beside the choice it clarifies, especially for networking, security, and persistence.
4. Preserve compact scanability, keyboard access, WCAG AA contrast, responsive behavior, and equivalent light/dark hierarchy.
5. Treat generated commands as safety-critical output: derive them from one authoritative manifest, surface invalid combinations before copy, and never imply unsupported capabilities.
