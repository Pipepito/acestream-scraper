# v2.3.0

Development preview of the bilingual TV experience, with three visual styles and shared viewing, recording and management workflows. Real-user testing and design iteration are ongoing; these notes do not announce a stable release.

## Added

- Choose English or Spanish and Lime, Coral/navy or Immersive cyan presentation independently of the light or dark theme.
- Organize recordings into collections and use reviewed drafts for recording, event, source, guide, account and connection changes.
- Use the standalone extraction helper in English or Spanish without connecting it to an installation.

## Improved

- Navigate through Watch, Recordings, Events, Channels, Connections, Settings and System, with WARP still easy to find.
- Keep playback, unsaved forms and pending actions when changing language, theme or presentation.
- Browse large channel catalogues and reorder the complete list with drag handles or keyboard controls while keeping rendering bounded.
- Review clearer status, error and confirmation messages across both languages and themes.

## Fixed

- Open recording forms over private-network HTTP when secure-context UUID APIs are unavailable, without crashing the page or creating a recording before confirmation.
- Keep phone reorder labels readable, clear obsolete administrative reads after disabling accounts, and serve application icons and manifest assets with their correct content types.

## Development testing

- Develop images are previews for real-device and real-user feedback. Screen-reader, native zoom, physical-device and fluent Spanish acceptance remain in progress across all three presentations.
- Report the image digest or commit, device, browser, language, theme, presentation, steps and expected versus actual behavior. Remove credentials and personal source details from reports.
- Back up persistent data before trying a development image and use a separate test installation where possible. Selecting v2.3.0 does not publish a version tag or change latest.
